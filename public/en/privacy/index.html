Privacy policy

Controller

Assense Software Solutions GmbH, Brandstwiete 46, 20457 Hamburg, Germany. Contact: kontakt@assense.com.

When you visit this site

To deliver the requested pages, the web server processes connection data such as your IP address, request time, requested URL and browser information. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The hosting setup determines whether these data are recorded in server logs, who hosts them and how long they are retained; these details must be completed before publication.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set cookies and does not include analytics, advertising scripts, externally hosted fonts or embedded third-party content. Images, fonts, styles and the appearance-selection script are served as part of this site.

Appearance preference

By default, the appearance follows your system setting. If you choose “Light” or “Dark”, we store only this choice under “assense-theme” in your browser’s local storage (localStorage). The setting is not transmitted to us and remains until you clear the site data or choose “System”. If local storage is blocked, your choice applies only to the current page.

Contact by email or telephone

If you contact us, we process the information you provide to respond to your request. Depending on its subject, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual or contractual communication) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (handling other inquiries). We retain correspondence as needed for its purpose and applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and, where applicable, data portability or object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Please contact us at the address above for requests concerning your data.
